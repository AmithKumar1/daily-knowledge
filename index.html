THE DECISION CHAIN

The voter-roll dispute is really a chain of decisions.

A voter list looks like a table of names. Administratively, it is a system—and every stage has a different responsibility.

Article 324 places superintendence, direction and control over electoral-roll preparation with the Election Commission. Statutory rules shape the process; field officers collect information; registration officers handle individual enrolment decisions; courts remain part of the review landscape.

The present argument sits on top of the Bihar SIR ordered in 2025 and considered by the Supreme Court in May 2026. The later national dispute is practical: roll changes, missing names, documentation, software, notices, and remedies available to people left out.
