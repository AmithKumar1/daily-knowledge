WHO ACTUALLY DECIDES WHETHER YOUR NAME STAYS ON INDIA'S VOTER LIST?

The Special Intensive Revision dispute is bigger than one headline number. It is a story about authority, field verification, documentation, software, and what happens when a name is missing.

By The Editorial Desk · Updated Oct. 2, 2026 7:00 AM IST

The Election Commission says jurisdictions where SIR has finished should run a special drive for eligible voters left out of the draft rolls, alongside young and first-time electors.

The 29 September instruction is carried here as an attributed item because the underlying communication was not directly retrieved during edition preparation.

The number circulating in the national argument is vast: reporting placed the total absent from draft rolls at roughly 13 crore across the completed SIR exercise. That figure is a draft-roll measure. It is not, by itself, evidence that 13 crore eligible voters were wrongly deleted.

LEAD STORYWHAT HAPPENED
THE DECISION CHAIN

The voter-roll dispute is really a chain of decisions.

A voter list looks like a table of names. Administratively, it is a system—and every stage has a different responsibility.

Article 324 places superintendence, direction and control over electoral-roll preparation with the Election Commission. Statutory rules shape the process; field officers collect information; registration officers handle individual enrolment decisions; courts remain part of the review landscape.

The present argument sits on top of the Bihar SIR ordered in 2025 and considered by the Supreme Court in May 2026. The later national dispute is practical: roll changes, missing names, documentation, software, notices, and remedies available to people left out.
