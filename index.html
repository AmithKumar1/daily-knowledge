WHO ACTUALLY DECIDES WHETHER YOUR NAME STAYS ON INDIA'S VOTER LIST?

The Special Intensive Revision dispute is not only about missing names. It is about who can change an electoral roll, what evidence is used, how the decision travels from the field to an officer, and what a voter can do when a name is left out.

WHAT CHANGED

The Election Commission of India says states and Union Territories where the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) has finished should run a special drive to identify and enrol eligible voters who were left out, alongside young and first-time electors. The September 29 instruction is reported by established newsrooms; the original communication was not directly retrieved for this edition and is therefore not treated here as a directly verified primary record.

The number circulating in the debate is large: reporting placed the total absent from draft rolls at roughly 13 crore across the completed SIR exercise. That figure is a draft-roll measure. It is not, by itself, evidence that 13 crore eligible voters were wrongly deleted.

LEAD STORY · WHAT HAPPENED

The voter-roll dispute is really a chain of decisions.

READING TIME · 7 MIN

A voter list looks like a table of names. Legally and administratively, it is a system. The Constitution places superintendence, direction and control over the preparation of electoral rolls with the Election Commission. Parliament supplies statutory rules; field officers collect information; registration officers decide individual enrolment questions; courts remain part of the appeal and review landscape.

The current SIR dispute arose from the Commission's decision to intensify verification in Bihar in 2025 and then extend intensive revision beyond Bihar. The Supreme Court's 27 May 2026 judgment recorded the framework for the Bihar exercise and the arguments on both sides. The live 2026 dispute is later: it concerns roll changes, documentation, software, omitted names and what the re-entry route means for people already left out.

The public question is not simply “how many names disappeared?” It is: what happened to each category of name, under which rule, through which officer, and with what remedy?

THE LONG VIEW

Every revision has a technical layer and a constitutional layer. The technical layer is forms, software, lists and field visits. The constitutional layer is inclusion, exclusion, franchise, procedure and review. Confusing the two makes the story harder to verify.

The practical issue for a voter is not the national argument in the abstract. It is whether their name is present, whether a claim is pending, whether a notice has been issued, which form or evidence applies, and where an appeal or correction can be made.
