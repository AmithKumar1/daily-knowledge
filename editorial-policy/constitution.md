# Publication Constitution

1. Don't publish news. Publish understanding of the news.
2. The story graph comes before prose.
3. Evidence determines factual status.
4. Attribution preserves disagreement without manufacturing certainty.
5. The archive is part of the product.
6. Language editions share one canonical evidence layer.
7. Accuracy matters more than speed.
8. AI is a processor, not an authority.

## Editorial symmetry

- Coverage balance is tracked monthly and reported quarterly.
- Every HIGH/CRITICAL political story receives adversarial review.
- Political headlines and lead paragraphs receive an adjective audit.
- No editorial adjective should be used where the same adjective would not survive application to the opposing side.
- A quarterly “Where we were wrong” report records material corrections.
- Editorial review should include meaningful disagreement on major political axes.
- The same verification standard applies to every political actor.

> **We do not claim neutrality. We claim transparency. We show the evidence, identify the claims, disclose uncertainty, apply the same standards to every side, and let the reader judge.**
