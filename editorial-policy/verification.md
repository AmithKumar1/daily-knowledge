# Verification Policy

## Status taxonomy

### VERIFIED_FACT
Supported by a directly checked primary source, or by two genuinely independent credible sources when no decisive primary record exists.

### ATTRIBUTED_CLAIM
A source, person or institution made the claim. The publication preserves the attribution rather than upgrading it to fact.

### DISPUTED
Credible evidence materially conflicts, and the conflict is itself part of the story.

### ANALYSIS
Interpretation built from established facts. Analysis must not be written as if it were documentary fact.

### UNKNOWN
The evidence available to the edition is insufficient for a reliable factual conclusion.

### DEVELOPING
The facts are changing materially and the edition should make the time boundary explicit.

## Source rules

Tier 1 sources establish the strongest factual record. Tier 2 sources provide independent reporting and discovery of context. Tier 3 sources deepen understanding. Tier 4 sources generate leads but are not proof.

Every high-materiality claim should carry source references. High-risk political, legal, court and breaking-news stories require human sign-off.
